# Privacy Policy — Berkeley Free Food Map

_Last updated: October 1, 2026_

Berkeley Free Food Map is a personal, non-commercial project that shows free food events at UC Berkeley on a map.

## What the app accesses
- **Gmail (read-only):** the project owner's own mailbox, used only to read messages labeled `FreeFood` (public club and department mailing lists). The app cannot send, modify, or delete email.
- **Public sources:** public event listings and public Instagram posts from campus organizations.

## What is stored
Only event details extracted from those messages: title, time, location, food, and sign-up link. Email bodies and personal information are not published or shared.

## Sharing
No data is sold or shared with third parties. Event text is sent to an AI model provider solely to extract event details.

## Revoking access
Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Open an issue at https://github.com/dmistry157/freeFood/issues.
